## Hand analytic question, not a formal character-sum theorem

For nonsingular short-Weierstrass E/Fp, p>3, subgroup H of order r, cyclic
field interval I of length L, let S={P in H excluding O | x(P) in I} and
alpha=L/p. For W in H excluding O, the candidate exact-safe estimate is

    abs(card(S intersect(W-S)) - (r-2)*alpha^2)
       <= 8*sqrt(p)*(1+ln p)^2.

Expand the two interval indicators in field characters. Nontrivial frequency
pairs use f=a*x(P)+b*x(W-P). When a,b are nonzero, distinct double poles at
O,W prevent cancellation and Artin-Schreier degeneracy (p>3), degree4.
Kohel/Shparlinski Corollary1 gives8sqrt(p) on the subgroup. With only one
nonzero frequency, subtracting the extra excluded point costs at most1,
absorbed by8sqrt(p). The all-trivial term has r-2 points. Their interval
Fourier-L1 bound gives the displayed remainder. Check every step and label
it hand analysis. At W=O symmetry gives the older single-gate density.

A useful constant lower bound of order1/b^2 requires r/b^2 to dominate
sqrt(p)*log(p)^2. Do not assert it for arbitrary tiny subgroups. No uniform
multilevel yield or efficient reporting follows from this estimate alone.
Representation multiplicities, deduplication, compatible tags and all states
must be charged; preserving every original partial tuple can itself force
large explicit lists. A target-fixed retention theorem is not an output-law
or retained-list-size theorem for an algorithm's selected states.
