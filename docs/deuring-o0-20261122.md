## Not formalized (and why)

- **Maximality of O₀.** It follows from `disc(O₀) = p = disc(B_{p,∞})` and the theorem that
  an order whose discriminant equals that of the algebra is maximal (Voight, Quaternion
  Algebras, Thm 15.5.5). Mathlib has no orders, discriminants or ramification of quaternion
  algebras.
- **End(E₀) ≅ O₀ as rings.** This needs endomorphisms as morphisms of curves, the group law
  on `WeierstrassCurve.Affine.Point`, and `π² = [-p]`. `E0Maps` proves the defining
  relations on coordinates only.
- **The Deuring correspondence itself** (ideals ↔ isogenies, `deg = nrd`). It is checked
  at toy size by Otto certificates (`isogeny-labs/otto` `examples/sqisign`,
  `check_deuring_endo_count.py`): `#{α ∈ O₀ : nrd α = ℓ} = 4·#{ℓ-isogenies E₀ → E₀}`.
